02 — Research

Research

An overview of my current and past projects, spanning ex-situ physiology experiments to skeletal geochemistry.

01

Current Projects

Coral Physiology

Boundary layer [H⁺] and O₂ under varying temperature

Measuring how temperature shapes the chemical microenvironment — specifically H⁺ and O₂ concentrations — at the coral boundary layer.

Calcification

[Ca²⁺] or [CO₃²⁻] control on Stylophora pistillata calcification and physiology

Decoupling calcium and carbonate ion concentrations to isolate their individual effects on respiration, calcification, and symbiont photosynthesis.

02

Past Projects

Coral Physiology

Temperature & alkalinity effects on S. pistillata and P. damicornis

Ex-situ experiments examining how elevated temperature and alkalinity interact to affect respiration, calcification, symbiont photosynthesis, and transcriptomics across two Indo-Pacific coral species.

Foraminifera · Geochemistry

Planktic foraminifera diurnal physiology and Mg/Ca incorporation

Diel rate measurements of Orbulina universa photosynthesis throughout the day and implications for Mg banding and Mg/Ca proxy interpretation.
