About

I'm a PhD candidate at The University of Cambridge, Department of Earth Sciences, supervised by Oscar Branson. I'm interested in the physiological processes that shape calcifying marine organism response to past, present, and future climate conditions. I try to answer these questions by using ex-situ experiments to measure coral and foraminifera physiological responses (respiration, calcification, symbiont, and transcriptomic) to variable ocean chemistry conditions.

I'm passionate about basic science.
